Day 1

2026 · AUG 10

This is my first day writing in English, and I'm doing it to improve my skills and to organize my mind. It's not easy when you have a lot of thoughts in your head, but that's what this exercise is for: to put everything in order and to show the things I can do…

Please be gentle with me, and enjoy this place. I promise I'll try to be fun and get a smile out of you. If that happens, the reason for this whole project will be paid off…

This journal will be about the stories I've lived — my own personal history, and things other people have told me. For that reason, I'll write in the third person, and you'll never know whether the character in the story is me or someone else.

You'll also see photos and things I used to do as a hobby. Remember, this is only for fun — every story and action here could be science fiction.
